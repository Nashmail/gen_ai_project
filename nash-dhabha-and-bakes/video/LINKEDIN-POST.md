What if your dhaba had an AI waiter? 🍛🤖

For my Introduction to Gen AI course project, I built Masala Mitra, a voice-enabled RAG chatbot for NASH Dhabha & Bakes.

🎙 Tell it your mood by voice or text, and it suggests dishes from the real menu
📖 RAG: it retrieves from 78 dishes and the restaurant's own info before answering, so it never makes up dishes or prices
👨‍🍳 Leave a typed or voice note for the chef on any dish
📦 Live order tracking, a bill with GST, and payment, all inside the chat
🌐 Node.js + Express backend, with an offline mode that runs the same engine in the browser

I tested it like a real product: 84 test steps in a real browser, including XSS and prompt-injection attempts, a server crash mid-order, two customers ordering at once, Hinglish input, and voice notes. Testing found 19 failing steps, and I fixed every one.
