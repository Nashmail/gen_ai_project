How ordering works

Masala Mitra stays with you from the first hello to the bill.

  1. Tell your moodSay hi, share your name and how you feel. Mitra suggests dishes from the menu.
  2. Build your orderAdd dishes from the chat or the menu. Veg and non-veg are clearly marked.
  3. Talk to the chefLeave a chef note on any dish, typed or as a voice note.
  4. Track, pay, rateMitra tells you when it's cooking and ready, then shares the bill and payment.

Connect Google Gemini

Masala Mitra always searches our menu first. With Gemini connected, Gemini writes the reply from those dishes, so it understands any way you ask. Every answer is checked: if Gemini names a dish or price that isn't on our menu, the built-in answer is used instead.
